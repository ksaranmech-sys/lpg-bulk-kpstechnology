## Full description (max 4000)
LPG Fleet Driver is the companion app for the KPS Technology fleet-management system used by LPG bulk
transport companies. It is for drivers and fleet administrators whose company has an account — there is no
public sign-up.

For drivers
• Start a trip and record loading and unloading details
• Log diesel fills with a receipt photo and automatic location tag
• Record advances, RTO charges, parking, cleaner and other expenses with photos
• Close the trip and see the settlement instantly
• View document expiry reminders for your vehicle and submit leave entries

For fleet administrators
• See open trips and trip history for every vehicle
• Manage vehicles, drivers and document expiry dates
• Calculate monthly driver salary and share the salary statement as a PDF
• Manage leave entries and route KM tables

Your login is created by your company's administrator. Contact them for access.
