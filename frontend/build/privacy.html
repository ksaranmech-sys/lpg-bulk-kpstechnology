Privacy Policy — LPG Fleet Driver

Effective date: 20 September 2026  ·  Publisher: KPS Technology

LPG Fleet Driver (the "App") and the companion website at lpg-bulk.kpstechnology.in are a fleet-management tool provided by KPS Technology to transport companies ("Customers") and their employees. Accounts are created by the Customer's administrator; the App has no public sign-up and is not intended for the general public or for children.

How information is used and shared

  • Data is visible to you, to the administrators of the Customer you work for, and to KPS Technology staff who operate the service.
  • Data is used only to run the fleet-management service (trip settlement, salary statements, document-expiry reminders). It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with third parties for their own purposes.
  • Service providers that store or transmit data on our behalf: MongoDB Atlas (database), Render (application server), Vercel (website), Cloudinary (photo storage) and our email provider (settlement reports and password reset codes). Each processes data solely under our instructions.

Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored as one-way hashes. Login sessions are stored in the device's secure keychain and expire automatically. Access is restricted by role: a driver can only see their own vehicle's trips.

Retention and deletion

Trip records are kept for one year after a trip is closed, then deleted automatically. Account data is kept while the account is active. To have an account or its data deleted, contact your employer's administrator or KPS Technology at the address below; deletion is completed within 30 days.

Permissions the App requests

  • Camera — to photograph receipts and meters.
  • Location — to tag diesel fills with the place of purchase.
  • Internet — to send data to the service.

You can withdraw camera or location permission at any time in your phone's settings; diesel entries require a location, so that feature will not work without it.
